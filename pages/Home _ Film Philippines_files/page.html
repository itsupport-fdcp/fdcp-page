FILIPIÑANA LOCAL PREMIERE AT CINEMALAYA 2026

After acclaimed international premieres at the Berlin International Film Festival and the Sundance Film Festival, Filipiñana finally comes home as the Closing Film of the 22nd Cinemalaya Philippine Independent Film Festival.

Set in an exclusive country club, the film follows Isabel, a young employee whose fascination with the club president leads her to uncover the darkness beneath its luxurious façade.

FDCP celebrates Lawrence Fajardo’s Silenced Nights for making history as the first Filipino film selected to compete in TIFF’s Platform program, the festival’s juried section dedicated to bold and distinctive filmmaking.

A recipient of the Film Philippines Office’s International Co-Production Fund (ICOF), Silenced Nights highlights the strength of Filipino filmmaking and the opportunities created through inter...national collaboration.

The winner of the Platform competition receives the Platform Prize and becomes eligible for consideration at the Academy Awards for Best International Feature Film.
