THE FDCP FILMPHILIPPINES OFFICE IS NOW ACCEPTING APPLICATIONS FOR THE FILMPHILIPPINES INCENTIVES PROGRAM CYCLE 3 OF 2021!

Interested production companies may apply for the following programs and avail its accompanying incentives:

Film Location Incentive Program (FLIP) - eligible international projects may receive a 20% cash rebate, capped at PHP 10 million, on their qualified spend in the Philippines

International Co-production Fund (ICOF) - Filipino producers who are working with international co-productions may receive support of up to PHP 10 million when they conduct production, animation or post-production in the Philippines

ASEAN Co-production Fund (ACOF) - Filipino producers who are working with ASEAN co-producers when they do part of their production, animation and/or post-production in the Philippines may receive support from USD 50,000 up to USD 150,00

Through these incentives programs, the @fdcpofficial, through its FilmPhilippines Office, aims to provide funding for domestic and international efforts and partnerships for Filipino film producers to serve as line producers, partners or co-producers for the production of quality projects.

CALLING ALL DOMESTIC AND FOREIGN PRODUCTIONS LOOKING TO FILM, ANIMATE, OR DO POST-PRODUCTION IN THE PHILIPPINES

The FDCP FilmPhilippines Office can help make things easier for you. Through the Film Location Engagement Desk (FLEX), we assist local and foreign productions with inter-agency coordination for government permits, locations, and production logistics in the Philippines.

The FLEX program of the FDCP FilmPhilippines is a one-stop-shop providing general endorsement and logistics assistance to productions in their transactions with government offices across the country and engagement with Philippine production companies, technical suppliers, and crew.

Application is open all year round. Interested production companies may apply by sending an email to filmphilippines@fdcp.ph.

Let's create together and explore the endless opportunities waiting for you in the Philippines. #FilmPhilippines

#FilmPhilippines Advisory: The Philippines on October 15 announced the updated list of "green, yellow, and red" countries, jurisdictions, and territories effective from October 16 to October 31.

Fully vaccinated individuals from “green” countries, territories, and jurisdictions are no longer required to undergo a facility-based quarantine provided that they present a negative RT-PCR test result taken at least 72 hours before their arrival in the Philippines. They are also advised to self-monitor for any symptoms until the 14th day.

Fully vaccinated individuals from "yellow" countries are required to undergo a facility-based quarantine until they yield a negative RT-PCR test result taken on the 5th day. They are then advised to home quarantine until the 10th day.

Unvaccinated, partially vaccinated, or individuals whose vaccination status cannot be independently verified or confirmed as valid or authentic by local authorities coming from “green” or “yellow” countries will undergo facility-based quarantine until they yield a negative RT-PCR test result.

Meanwhile, anyone who has a travel history to a country listed in the “red” category will be denied entry to the country regardless of vaccination status.

Filipinos returning to the country via government-initiated repatriation, non-government-initiated repatriation, and Bayanihan flights may be allowed entry subject to testing and quarantine protocols.

FDCP FilmPhilippines and Safe Filming PH Joint Advisory

Last September 29, the Film Development Council of the Philippines (FDCP) presented to the Inter-Agency Task Force (IATF) Technical Working Group (TWG) a proposal to shorten the quarantine of fully vaccinated audiovisual workers on the condition of the worker being able to show proof of vaccination and a negative RT-PCR test result taken at most 72 hours before their flight.

As announced by Malacañang, effective October 14, fully vaccinated Filipinos and foreigners are no longer required to do facility-based quarantine as long as they come from a "green" country. They must present however a negative RT-PCR test result taken at least three days prior to their departure from their country of origin.

Fully vaxxed individuals are still enjoined to self-monitor for any symptoms until the 14th day.

On the other hand, facility-based quarantine is still required for unvaccinated and partially vaccinated individuals and those whose vaccination status cannot be verified until their RT-PCR test taken on the 5th day of quarantine yields a negative result.

The FDCP welcomes this development as this would help encourage foreign productions to shoot their projects in the Philippines which can provide opportunities for our local audiovisual workers.

In addition, the National Capital Region will shift to Alert Level 3 effective October 16, following the pilot implementation of the Alert Levels System for the country's COVID-19 response.

All local film and audiovisual shoots are allowed to operate with a maximum of 50 people on-site for all alert levels, subject to the same restrictions and protocols of the DTI-DOLE-DOH Joint Administrative Order (JAO) No. 2021-0001. #FilmPhilippines #SafeFilmingPH
