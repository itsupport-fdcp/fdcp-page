10 Funtastic Reasons

To Film in the Philippines

  • 01

    Lots of locations with character, showcased on an online database

  • 02

    High-quality and cost-efficient equipment and facilities

  • 03

    Government National Registry database of skilled English-speaking talents, crews, and companies

  • 04

    Your Filipino partner’s attitude of flexibility and ingenuity, to be selected amongst the FilmPhilippines recommended companies

  • 05

    Ease of business with a one-stop shop for permits, visas, and tax-free importation with FLEX

  • 06

    A 20% cash rebate on your eligible local spending with FLIP

  • 07

    Up to 200,000 USD for co-productions of feature films with ICOF

  • 08

    Up to 150,000 USD for regional co-productions of feature films

  • 09

    International health and safety protocols with Safe Filming

  • 10

    Home to all stories with no restrictions and welcoming endless production possibilities in a reliable environment
