# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Filipino and regional film and television professionals, cultural institutions, media partners, filmmakers, and members of the public seeking authoritative information about FDCP programs and events.

## Product Purpose

Present FDCP initiatives and events clearly, credibly, and accessibly while supporting participation, registration, and regional cultural collaboration.

## Brand Personality

Institutional, cinematic, and welcoming. The experience should feel confident and culturally grounded without becoming visually bureaucratic or difficult to scan.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic corporate event templates, dense government-portal layouts, decorative effects that compete with campaign artwork, and fixed desktop canvases that break on smaller screens.

## Design Principles

- Let official campaign artwork carry the visual identity.
- Make event information easy to read and navigate at every viewport size.
- Preserve FDCP brand consistency while respecting each campaign's art direction.
- Favor robust, Drupal-safe implementation over fragile decorative techniques.
- Use clear hierarchy and concise copy to support participation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain readable type, sufficient contrast, useful alternative text, keyboard-accessible controls, and responsive layouts that do not require horizontal page scrolling. Respect reduced-motion preferences whenever motion is introduced.
