Lights, Camera, Collaboration:
Welcome to the ASEAN Film & Television Summit 2026!

This August, the heart of Southeast Asian storytelling beats in Metro Manila. Organized by the Film Development Council of the Philippines (FDCP) in close collaboration with the Asian Film Alliance Network (AFAN), this landmark summit will take place on August 12–14, 2026, running in conjunction with the prestigious ASEAN Summit 2026. Under the guiding theme, “Navigating our Future, Together,” this premier event serves as a dynamic bridge for national film bodies, producers, streamers, cultural institutions, and industry leaders from across Asia. Together, attendees will dive into a rich lineup of forward-thinking talks, high-level panel discussions, the vital AFAN Roundtable, and curated networking activities designed to spark boundary-crossing collaborations. More than just a conference, the summit is a celebration of regional solidarity and creative synergy, offering immersive cultural engagements that honor our shared heritage while charting a bold, unified path forward for the global future of Asian cinema and television.

ASEAN FILM & TELEVISION SUMMIT
August 14, 2026 | Philippines

The ASEAN Film & Television Summit 2026, organized by the Film Development Council of the Philippines (FDCP) in collaboration with the Asian Film Alliance Network (AFAN), will take place on August 12–14, 2026 in Metro Manila, Philippines, in conjunction with the ASEAN Summit 2026.

TOPIC 1:
The Cross-Media Integration of Asian Content and
Liquid IP (Intellectual Property)

This discussion will focus on explaining the horizontal chain of value and finding ways to utilize intellectual properties in different media, including brand and content creation and distribution. This may include film, comics, literature, video games and other visual works. Treating IPs as evolving and adaptive assets opens up a new perspective and business model of selling and distributing films and content, revisiting production methodologies, and maximizing the income-generating potential of every original work. A case study may be conducted to portray its possibilities and challenges.

Discussion points:

  • Challenges in the current chain of value of film production
  • Cross-media integration and utilization of IPs
  • Successful integration, IP-based brand, content creation and distribution
